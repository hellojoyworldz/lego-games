How to Play

Basic Rules

  • Tap marbles to select or deselect them.
  • If the selected numbers add up to exactly 10, they pop!
  • Go over 10 and you fail — your combo breaks.

Score & Combo

  • Each success restores score and time.
  • Chain successes to build combo bonuses.
  • Clear every marble for a Perfect Clear bonus!

Game Over

  • The game ends when time hits 0.
  • If marbles stay above the danger line for 3 seconds, you lose.

Controls

  • 💡 Hint See a combo that makes 10
  • ＋ Balls Drop 2 marbles from above
  • 🔄 Shuffle Mix remaining marble positions
  • ⏸ Pause Pause or quit the game
